### About the Experiment 

In this experiment, the quality of butter oil/ghee will be evaluated by determining important physicochemical parameters such as free fatty acid content, moisture content, and other standard fat constants. These tests help in assessing purity, freshness, rancidity, and compliance with food quality standards. Quality testing ensures consumer safety, prevents adulteration, and maintains product authenticity. The experiment also provides practical exposure to analytical techniques used in dairy quality control and food safety assurance.
