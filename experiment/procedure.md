Requirements (Instruments, Chemicals & Other)<br>

Material Required:<br>
1. Polenske flask (310 ± 10 ml volume)<br>
2. Graduated cylinders (25 ml and 100 ml)<br>
3. Pipette (50 ml)<br>
4. Distillation apparatus (Polenske distillation setup)<br>
5. Burette (50 ml, graduated to 0.1 ml)<br>
6. Conical flasks (250 ml)<br>
7. Glass funnel<br>
8. Whatman filter paper (Grade 4)<br>
  
Reagents:<br>
1. Glycerol (98%)<br>
2. Sodium hydroxide solution (50%)<br>
3. Dilute sulfuric acid (1:1)<br>
4. Ethanol (95%)<br>
5. Phenolphthalein indicator (0.5% solution in ethanol)<br>
6. 0.1N Sodium hydroxide or Barium hydroxide solution<br>
7. Silver sulphate<br>

Procedure <br>

Saponification Steps:<br><br>
	1. Take two 300 ml distilling flasks marked as blank (B) and sample (S) and add 5 g of ghee into the “S” flask.<br>
	2. Add 20 g of glycerol and 2 ml of 0.5N NaOH solution in both the flask.<br>
	3. Heat both the flask over the heating mantle with stirring until the sample is completely saponified and clear.<br>
  
Distillation Steps:<br><br>
	4. Add 93 ml of boiling distilled water in both the flask and dissolve properly.<br>
	5. Add 50 ml of 1.0 N sulfuric acid in both the flask<br>
	6. Connect the flask “S” to the distillation apparatus.<br>
	7. Distil 110 ml of distillate in 19-21 minutes.<br>
	8. Connect the flask “B” to the distillation apparatus and collect 110 ml distillate<br>
  
Filtration and Titration:<br><br>
	9. Filter both the distillate separately through Whatman filter paper (Grade 4).<br>
	10. Take out 100 ml filtrate of the flask “S” in a conical flask and titrate with 0.1N NaOH using 0.1 ml of phenolphthalein indicator until a persistent pink color appears (R.M. Value). (T1)<br>
	11. Take out 100 ml filtrate of the flask “B” in a conical flask and titrate with 0.1N NaOH using 0.1 ml of phenolphthalein indicator until a persistent pink color appears (R.M. Value). (T3)<br>
	12. Wash the residue of flask “S” with 50 ml of hot 95% ethanol and titrate with 0.1N NaOH using 0.1 ml of phenolphthalein indicator to determine the Polenske value. (T2)<br>
13. Wash the residue of flask “B” with 50 ml of hot 95% ethanol and titrate with 0.1N NaOH using 0.1 ml of phenolphthalein indicator to determine the Polenske value. (T4)<br>
14.	Calculations<br>
Reicher-Meissl value=1.10 (T1-T2)<br>
Polenske value=(T3-T4)<br>
Where, <br>
T1 = Volume (ml) of 0.1N NaOH used for the sample for Reicher-Meissl value<br>
T2 = Volume (ml) of 0.1N NaOH used for blank.<br>
T3 = Volume (ml) of 0.1N NaOH used for the sample for Polenske value<br>
T4 = Volume (ml) of 0.1N NaOH used for blank.<br>
